0. The bet

The case for Granola at this price rests on four beliefs, and it is worth stating them before the evidence so the evidence can be judged against them.1

The first is that Granola has genuinely won the first act of its market — standalone AI meeting notes — and won it on demand rather than on packaging. Ramp’s procurement data, updated the day this was written, shows Granola paid for by 64 percent of the businesses buying in its category3, up 34 points in a year … If the first data covering the period after Google switched its own notes on by default shows that share falling, this belief is wrong.2

The third is that the $1.5 billion is a payment for the second act … Pedregal has been unusually candid that this was always the plan: “the product we have today is a Trojan horse to collect a lot of your context,” he told Matt Turck in August 2025 (36:55)4.
