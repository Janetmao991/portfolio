RAG

A customer asks: “I’m a member, I bought a chair 10 days ago. Can I still return it?” Ask an AI model with no documents and it can’t be sure, or it gets it wrong. RAG is the fix: first find the few relevant passages in your documents, then have the AI answer from them, and cite its sources.
